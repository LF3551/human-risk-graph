How the score is calculated

Model hrg-2.

  • Key-person risk K: the largest criticality × impact, where impact is the share of connections between the others that are lost without the person.
  • Approval concentration D: Herfindahl-Hirschman index of approval weight; 1/D is the effective number of approvers.
  • Override exposure B: weighted share of critical approvals whose approver can be bypassed.

Composite risk = (1 − (1 − 0.4·K)(1 − 0.3·D)(1 − 0.3·B)) / (1 − (1 − 0.4)(1 − 0.3)(1 − 0.3)) = 0.476; score = 100·(1 − risk) = 52
